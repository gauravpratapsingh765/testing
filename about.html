Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Everything we do is guided by a clear purpose — to empower every student to reach their highest potential, morally and academically.

Our Vision

To be a nationally recognised centre of academic excellence, where every student, regardless of background, is equipped to compete at the highest level, lead with integrity, and build a future without limits.

We envision an institution where board toppers and IIT/NEET selections are not exceptions; they are the standard. Where disciplined minds meet compassionate hearts, and where every child who walks through our doors leaves as a confident, capable, and nationally competitive individual.

Our Mission

To nurture each student with the care of a teacher and the commitment of a mentor — delivering excellence in both board education and competitive preparation, so that quality learning is never a privilege, but a promise.

We are committed to:

  • ● Making premium education accessible to students from all walks of life
  • ● Providing personal attention and mentoring at every stage of learning
  • ● Maintaining the highest academic standards through experienced, dedicated faculty
  • ● Nurturing not just scholars, but responsible and driven human beings

Our Journey

The Story So Far

From humble beginnings to a premier multi-branch institution — here is how Sri Sai Inter College has grown.

2006 — Founding Year

🏫 The Foundation Stone

In 2006, with a deep belief that every child deserves quality education, Sri Sai Inter College opened its doors in Lakhpedabagh, Barabanki. It began as a single wing, modest in size, but uncompromising in purpose. Teach well, care deeply, and never give up on a student.

2011 — Landmark Moment

🏆 Rank 1 in Uttar Pradesh

Five years of dedication produced a historic result. Gyan Prakash Verma secured Rank 1 in the entire UP Board examination — proof that a school in Barabanki could compete with anyone, anywhere.

2021 — New Chapter

⚛️ The IIT/NEET Premium Wing

Fifteen years of trust earned, Sri Sai launched a dedicated IIT-JEE and NEET preparation wing at a brand new campus — expert faculty, a structured program, and a dream that had grown bigger.

2023 — Digital Era

💻 A Modern Learning Experience

Sri Sai introduced smart classrooms and digital learning tools across both branches, modernising the experience while keeping the personal attention and mentoring that have always defined the institution.

Today & Beyond

🚀 Growing Stronger Every Year

Today, Sri Sai stands as a multi-branch institution with over 50 IIT/NEET selections, a growing family of board toppers, and thousands of students whose lives have been shaped within these walls. The best chapters are still being written.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These principles are the backbone of everything we teach, practise, and believe in at Sri Sai Inter College.

Excellence

We set high standards and push every student to discover and exceed their own limits, academically and personally. At Sri Sai, ordinary effort is the starting point, not the ceiling.

Mentorship

Every teacher here is more than an instructor — they are a guide, a motivator, and sometimes, the person who believes in a student before the student believes in themselves.

Compassion

We foster a culture of empathy, where teachers care for students as individuals, not just roll numbers. Every child's struggle is seen, and every child's progress is celebrated.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct are non-negotiable in the classroom, the exam hall, and beyond. We believe character is built long before a result is declared.

Rootedness

We are proud of where we come from. Our students carry the values of their families and communities as they compete and win nationally.

Discipline

Structure, punctuality, and respect for process are not rules imposed from outside — they are habits built from within, because consistency separates good students from great ones.

The People Behind It All

Our Founders

Sri Sai Inter College was built on the vision, sacrifice, and relentless dedication of two individuals who believed that quality education can transform lives.

Mr Surendra Verma
Founder & Director
"A classroom should feel like a challenge and a comfort at the same time — that is where real learning happens."

With over 25 years of experience in education and an M.Sc., Mr Surendra Verma is the academic engine behind Sri Sai Inter College. His passion for teaching is not something he carries into the classroom — it is something students feel the moment he walks in. Today, he leads the IIT-JEE and NEET wing with the same energy and conviction he brought to the very first batch in 2006. For him, every selection is personal.

M.Sc. 25+ Years Experience
Mr Navneet Tiwari
Founder & Director
"Every student understands, you just have to find the right way to explain. I have never met a child who cannot learn."

Before Sri Sai Inter College, Mr Navneet Tiwari and Mr Surendra Verma ran NSD Classes, one of the most respected coaching centres in the region. With an M.Sc. and 25+ years of teaching behind him, Mr Tiwari built Sri Sai on one enduring truth: the students who need a teacher the most are rarely the ones at the top of the class. His style is simple, detailed, and deeply patient — he breaks down every concept until every student in the room has understood it, not just the brightest few.
